About LazyCode AI

Understand code easily with your own local AI assistant.

What it does

LazyCode AI sends your snippet to a model running locally with Ollama and turns the answer into a readable breakdown: a summary, a step-by-step walkthrough, the expected output, and the key concepts involved. Everything runs on your machine — no code is sent to any external API.

How it works

  1. You paste code into the editor and pick a language.
  2. Flask forwards the snippet to your local Ollama model.
  3. The model answers in structured Markdown.
  4. The page renders it safely and saves it to your local history.
